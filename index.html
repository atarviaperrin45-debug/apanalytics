A Little About Me

Hello! I'm Atarvia Perrin, an aspiring data analytics professional and Master of Public Administration candidate at Clark Atlanta University, concentrating in Public Policy. I earned my Bachelor of Science in Human Services from Kennesaw State University, with a concentration in Nonprofit Management.

My passion lies at the intersection of data analytics, institutional research, higher education, and public policy. I enjoy transforming complex data into meaningful insights that help organizations improve performance, support student success, and make informed decisions.

Through my experiences in institutional research, information technology, and nonprofit organizations, I've developed a foundation in data analysis, research, visualization, and program evaluation. I have experience using Power BI, Tableau, Excel, and R to explore datasets, develop dashboards, and communicate meaningful findings.

Beyond academics and professional development, I am involved in the National Society of Black Engineers (NSBE) and Black Women in Data, communities that encourage professional growth, networking, and representation in technology and analytics.

Looking ahead, I aspire to pursue a Master of Science in Analytics to strengthen my quantitative and technical expertise. My long-term goal is to bridge the gap between data, policy, and organizational strategy, using analytics to drive meaningful change and improve outcomes for organizations and communities.
