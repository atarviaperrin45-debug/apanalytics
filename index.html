A Little About Me

Where Data, Policy & Purpose Meet ✨

I'm Atarvia Perrin, an aspiring data analytics professional and Master of Public Administration candidate at Clark Atlanta University, concentrating in Public Policy. I earned my Bachelor of Science in Human Services from Kennesaw State University, with a concentration in Nonprofit Management.

My passion lies at the intersection of data analytics, institutional research, higher education, and public policy. I enjoy transforming complex data into meaningful insights that improve organizational performance, support student success, and inform strategic decision-making.

Through my experiences in institutional research, information technology, and nonprofit organizations, I've developed skills in research, data analysis, visualization, and program evaluation. I have experience using Power BI, Tableau, Excel, and R to analyze information, develop dashboards, and communicate findings.

I am involved in the National Society of Black Engineers (NSBE) and Black Women in Data, communities that encourage professional growth, networking, and representation in technology and analytics.

Looking ahead, I aspire to pursue a Master of Science in Analytics to strengthen my quantitative and technical expertise. My long-term goal is to connect data, policy, and organizational strategy to drive meaningful change.
